Communication job

By the end, a mixed nontechnical AI audience should recognize that AI amplifies
longstanding problems of context, provenance, reuse, and handoff; understand
that CON works on those problems through open community infrastructure; and
remember STAMPED as seven useful questions for making digital work intelligible
later.

Narrative

1. A knowledge worker has an extremely productive AI session.
2. The next morning, they cannot recover what happened or continue the work.
3. Science has lived with the same fragmentation across data, code, methods,
   metadata, environments, and human memory for decades.
4. Open science, standards, repositories, versioning, and workflows are
   community responses to that older problem.
5. CON contributes open, reusable, tested, convenient community infrastructure.
6. AI lets the team build faster and can also manufacture confusion faster.
7. STAMPED supplies a tool-agnostic vocabulary for better research objects.
8. Seven playful questions connect each formal principle to ordinary digital
   work.
9. The close generalizes beyond neuroscience: build the memory into the work.

Visual direction

Rapid Lessig-style pacing; warm cream, ink navy, teal, coral, and mustard;
large type; surprising scale changes; occasional full-bleed editorial collage;
very little conventional bullet content. Technical definitions appear in a
quiet bottom strip and are not presented as material to memorize.

Output

- Editable PowerPoint deck with speaker notes and source blocks.
- Public static web deck using rendered slide images and keyboard navigation.
- Concise speaker-notes document for rehearsal.
